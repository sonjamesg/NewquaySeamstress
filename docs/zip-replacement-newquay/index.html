Zip Replacement & Buttons in Newquay

Trouser, skirt, jeans, coat and jacket zips replaced, buttons restitched and new machine buttonholes. Guide prices from £3.

Broken zip? Do not throw it away

A broken zip is the most common reason a favourite coat or pair of jeans ends up at the back of the wardrobe, and it is one of the most satisfying repairs we do. A zip that is set in properly, with straight stitching and the teeth hidden where they should be, makes a garment feel new again.

Zip replacement we offer

  • Trouser and skirt zips: guide price includes a standard zip.
  • Jeans zips: guide price includes a heavy-duty metal zip, because jeans need one.
  • Coat and jacket zips: from the guide price plus the cost of the specialist zip (open-ended, two-way and chunky outdoor zips vary in length and type).
  • Cushion cover zips: see curtains & home.

Buttons and buttonholes

Lost a button or have one hanging by a thread? Button replacement or restitching is a guide £3 each, or 3 for £7. We can also add a new machine buttonhole, which is handy when a button has been moved after taking a waistband in.

Before you drop off

Please wash the garment first, as we only work on clean items. If you have a particular zip colour or the original spare button, bring it along. Standard turnaround is 3–5 days, or 24–48 hours express for an extra £5.
