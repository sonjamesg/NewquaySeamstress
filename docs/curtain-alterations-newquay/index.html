Curtains shortened to fit, without buying new

New windows, a move, or a bargain pair that is just a little too long: shortening curtains is far cheaper than replacing them, and done properly nobody can tell. We shorten unlined curtains and lined or blackout curtains, keeping hems straight, linings hanging correctly and weights in place.

Curtain and home services

  • Shorten unlined curtains: per pair, guide price from £20.
  • Shorten lined or blackout curtains: per pair, guide price from £32. Lining and face fabric are shortened separately.
  • Cushion cover zip replacement: guide £12, great for sofa cushions and holiday-let soft furnishings.

Measuring your curtains

Measure from the top of the curtain (or the hook position) to where you want the hem to finish, and note whether you want them to just clear the floor, sit on the sill or puddle. Please bring curtains freshly washed: we can only work on clean items, and washing first avoids shrinkage after shortening.

For holiday lets and second homes

Newquay, Crantock and Padstow are full of holiday lets with tired soft furnishings. Shortening curtains and replacing broken cushion zips between seasons is an easy way to freshen a property. Standard turnaround is 3–5 days, or express 24–48 hours for an extra £5.

Guide prices for this service

Guide prices only. Every garment is different, so your final price is confirmed at drop-off or consultation, depending on the garment and the work needed. Nothing is started until you have agreed the price.
