# Frequently Asked Questions

> Answers on alteration guide prices, 3–5 day turnaround, 24–48 hour express, washing, pinning hems, zips and curtain shortening in Newquay.

Source: https://newquayseamstress.com/faq/

FAQ

Prices, turnaround, washing, pinning and how to book. Everything people ask before bringing work in.

How much do alterations cost in Newquay?

Our [guide prices](https://newquayseamstress.com/prices/) start from £3 for a button and £10 for a standard jeans hem. Trouser hems are a guide £12, zip replacements from £15 and curtain shortening from £20 per pair. These are guide prices only: the final price is confirmed at drop-off or consultation, depending on the garment and the work needed.

How long do alterations take?

Standard turnaround is 3–5 days. If you need it sooner, our express service turns most jobs around in 24–48 hours for an extra £5.

Do my clothes or curtains need to be washed first?

Yes please. We can only work on clean items, so garments and curtains must be freshly washed before drop-off. It keeps the workroom (and everyone else's clothes) clean and means hems are measured on fabric that has already shrunk. No washing machine big enough for your curtains? Our friends at [Newquay Launderama](https://newquaylaunderama.com/) offer service washes and large-item washing, so they can get them clean for you first.

Can you pin my hem when I drop off?

Yes. We are happy to pin hems at drop-off, so bring the shoes you will wear with trousers or a dress. Alternatively you can pin or measure your preferred length at home and we will work to that.

Can you shorten jeans and keep the original hem?

Yes. A jeans hem keeping the original factory hem (so the worn, faded edge stays) has a guide price of £15. A standard jeans hem is a guide £10.

Do zip replacements include the zip?

Trouser and skirt zip replacement (guide £15) includes a standard zip, and jeans zip replacement (guide £18) includes a heavy-duty metal zip. Coat and jacket zips start from £25 plus the cost of the specialist zip, as these vary in length and type.

Is there a minimum charge?

Yes, the minimum repair charge is £8. Buttons are the exception: replacing or restitching a button is a guide £3, or 3 for £7.

Do you shorten curtains, including lined and blackout curtains?

Yes. Shortening unlined curtains starts from £20 per pair and lined or blackout curtains from £32 per pair. We also replace cushion cover zips (guide £12).
