Suit Tailoring & Formal Wear in Newquay

Military-standard tailoring for suits, jackets, wedding suits and formal wear, from a tailor who has made suits from scratch. Price on consultation.

Book a consultationGuide prices

Suit tailoring with a military tailor's eye

For more than 50 years Sylvia worked as a tailor for the military at RAF St Mawgan, where uniforms are inspected and nothing short of immaculate passes. She has also made suits from scratch. That combination of discipline and construction knowledge is exactly what a good suit alteration needs.

Suits and formal wear

  • Suit trousers hemmed, with plain, blind-stitched or turn-up finishes (see trouser guide prices)
  • Jacket and suit fitting adjustments, assessed at a fitting
  • Wedding suits for grooms, groomsmen and fathers of the bride
  • Formal wear for balls, dinners, proms and events
  • Uniform and blazer alterations

Price on consultation

Trouser hems on suits follow our published guide prices. Jacket work and full suit tailoring are priced on consultation, because what is possible depends on how the jacket is constructed and how much fabric is in the seams. We will tell you honestly what will work.

Clean garments only, please. Get in touch to arrange a fitting.
