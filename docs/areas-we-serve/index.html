Areas we serve

Seamstress for Newquay & Surrounding Areas

We are based in Newquay and welcome drop-offs and fittings from across mid and north Cornwall.

  • Seamstress near Newquay

    Our home town. Drop-offs from Pentire, Porth, Lusty Glaze, Trenance, Tretherras and the town centre.

  • Seamstress near St Columb Major

    A short drive inland along the A39 corridor, ideal for hems and zips on the way into town.

  • Seamstress near St Mawgan & Mawgan Porth

    Close to RAF St Mawgan, where Sylvia spent her career as a military tailor.

  • Seamstress near Crantock

    Just across the Gannel. Popular for jeans hems and curtain shortening for holiday lets.

  • Seamstress near St Newlyn East

    A few miles south of Newquay, an easy hop for repairs and alterations.

  • Seamstress near Quintrell Downs

    On the doorstep. Bring trousers, jeans and coats with broken zips.

  • Seamstress near Perranporth

    Wetsuit-and-jeans country. Zip replacement and seam repairs are our everyday work.

  • Seamstress near St Austell

    Wedding, suit and ball gown consultations are worth the drive in from mid Cornwall.

  • Seamstress near Truro

    Cornwall's city. Customers come to us for tailoring and bridal alterations by appointment.

  • Seamstress near Padstow & Wadebridge

    Up the coast. Holiday-let curtains and cushion zips are a speciality worth the trip.

  • Seamstress near Indian Queens & St Dennis

    A quick run down the A30 for everyday alterations and mending.

  • Seamstress near Holywell Bay & Cubert

    Small jobs welcome, from a single button to a full set of trouser hems.
