Made-to-measure dressmaking and ball gowns in Newquay

Sylvia has made ball gowns, suits and wedding dresses, and brings over half a century of tailoring to every commission. With Maxine working alongside her, we can help bring a garment to life, from an idea or a favourite piece you would love copied.

What we make

  • Ball gowns and evening dresses
  • Formal and special-occasion wear
  • Made-to-measure garments designed with you
  • Remodelling or restyling a garment you already own

How a commission works

It starts with a conversation: what you want, when you need it, and the fabric you have in mind. We then take measurements and agree the design and price before anything is cut. Bespoke work is priced on consultation, since every piece is different.

Allow plenty of time for bespoke work, especially for balls and weddings, so there is room for fittings. Get in touch to talk it through.

Already have the dress? See wedding dress alterations or suit tailoring.

Price on consultation

Every gown and suit is different, so we price this work after a fitting and a proper look at the garment or design.
