We offer a free site survey, arranging a convenient time to visit your property and assess your requirements. This allows us to design your shutters to meet your exact aesthetic and functionality needs. We take the time to show you our range of styles, materials and colours. To save you time, we confirm all specifications and take final measurements in one visit. The next time we see you will be when we fit your beautiful bespoke plantation shutters.
