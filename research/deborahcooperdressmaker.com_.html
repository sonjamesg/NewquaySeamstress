About

Quality sewing, personally done

Deborah Cooper has run Quality Sewing from Redruth since 1990, specialising in alterations for wedding dresses and bridesmaids, alongside special occasion wear. Every garment is treated as one of a kind, because to the person wearing it, it is.

Quality Sewing is recognised by the Guild of Master Craftsmen with a Certificate of Quality & Service, awarded for high standards of customer care, product quality and presentation. Customers say the same: 147 five-star reviews on Google, and 100% of Facebook reviewers recommend.
