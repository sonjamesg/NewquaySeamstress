Trouser hems, jeans hems and fitting in Newquay

Trousers and jeans are the bread and butter of any sewing room, and they are where a tailor's training shows. A hem that sits a few millimetres off, a blind stitch that puckers, or a jeans hem that loses its faded edge are all things you notice every time you get dressed. Sylvia learned to hem uniform trousers to military standard at RAF St Mawgan, and that is how every pair is finished here.

{{img jeans-hemming-zip-repair-newquay|Denim jeans with a chalked hem line beside a brass zip, vintage buttons, tape measure and tailor's chalk}}

What we do

  • Standard jeans hem: shortened and re-stitched with matching heavy thread.
  • Jeans hem keeping the original factory hem: the worn, faded hem is kept and re-attached higher up, so your jeans look untouched.
  • Trouser hems, plain or blind stitch: an invisible finish for work trousers and smart trousers.
  • Lined trouser hems and turn-ups: lining and outer shortened separately so they hang properly.
  • Take in the waist or taper the legs: for standard trousers and for jeans, including heavier denim.

Getting the length right

We are happy to pin your hems at drop-off. Bring the shoes you will wear with them, because a trainer and a heeled boot need very different lengths. If it is easier, pin or measure your preferred length at home and we will work to that. Please bring trousers and jeans freshly washed: we only work on clean items, and pre-washed denim will not shrink after we have hemmed it.
