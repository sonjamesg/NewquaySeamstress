Mending that makes clothes last

The most sustainable garment is the one already in your wardrobe. A split seam, a small tear or a hole in a pocket lining is usually a quick fix, and a neat repair can add years to a jacket, a pair of work trousers or a much-loved jumper. Newquay is hard on clothes, from salt, sand and wetsuit-changing in car parks, and we see plenty of it.

Repairs we carry out

  • Split seam restitching: side seams, seat seams, underarms and crotch seams re-sewn and reinforced.
  • Small tear and patch repairs: neat repairs or patches on everyday clothes and workwear.
  • Pocket bag repair or replacement: worn-through trouser and coat pockets rebuilt so you stop losing your keys.
  • Buttons: replaced or restitched, guide £3 each or 3 for £7.

Our minimum repair charge is £8. If you are not sure whether something can be saved, bring it in. We would rather tell you honestly at drop-off than charge for a repair that will not last.
