A Military Tailor & Her Daughter

A military tailor with more than 50 years at the cutting table, and the daughter learning every stitch from her.

Fifty years of tailoring at RAF St Mawgan

Sylvia Pauline Tippett was a tailor at RAF St Mawgan, near Newquay, working for the military for over 50 years. Military tailoring is unforgiving: uniforms are worn on parade, inspected closely and expected to last. Hems must be level, seams straight and every detail regulation-correct. Half a century of that work builds a level of precision you can see in the finish.

Sylvia's skill reaches well beyond uniforms: over her career she has made ball gowns, suits and wedding dresses, and that understanding of how garments are constructed is what lets her alter them so well, from a simple pair of jeans to a layered bridal gown.

Mother and daughter

Maxine Jennings is Sylvia's daughter. For more than three years she has worked alongside her mum, learning her skills and standards at the sewing machine, so the craft continues in Newquay for the next generation of customers.
